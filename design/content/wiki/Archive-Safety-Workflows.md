# Archive safety workflows · 壓縮檔安全工作流程

`WinForge.exe --page archives` accepts bounded include/exclude masks and all NTFS time/access switches. Move mode creates, separately integrity-tests with password/split-volume awareness, then uses the Recycle Bin; arbitrary in-archive deletion shows exact validated masks before `7z d`.

`WinForge.exe --page archives` 接受有界 include／exclude 樣式同完整 NTFS 時間／存取開關。搬走模式會先建立、用支援密碼／分卷嘅獨立完整性測試，再用回收筒；任意檔內刪除會喺 `7z d` 前顯示準確已驗證樣式。
