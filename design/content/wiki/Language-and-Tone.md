# Language & Tone · 語言與語氣

![Funny-level settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ding-Ding-Projects/WinForge/main/docs/screenshot-funny-level-settings-live.png)

WinForge keeps its three persisted language modes—English, playful Hong Kong-style Cantonese, and compact bilingual—and lets you choose English and Cantonese playfulness independently from level 1 through level 5. Open **Settings**, then use **Funny level (tone)**. Changes apply live to explicitly authored safe copy and persist across restarts. · WinForge 保留英文、香港地道玩味粵語、精簡雙語三種持久語言模式，亦可以將英文同粵語玩味程度分開揀第 1 至第 5 級。去 **設定**，再用 **搞笑等級（語氣）**；改動會即時套用落明確寫好嘅安全文案，重開 app 亦會保留。

## Levels · 級別

- **1** — fully serious · 完全正經
- **2–4** — progressively warmer and more playful authored wording · 逐級更親切、更有玩味嘅明確文案
- **5** — most playful, while remaining respectful and accurate · 最玩得，但仍然尊重同準確

Bilingual mode combines the selected English variant with the independently selected Cantonese variant. The live preview is a polite accessibility live region, and both exact-step sliders provide keyboard instructions and visible numeric summaries. · 雙語模式會將目前英文版本同獨立揀選嘅粵語版本合併顯示。即時預覽係 polite 無障礙 live region；兩個準確步進 slider 都有鍵盤指示同可見數值摘要。

## Safety boundary · 安全界線

Funny level never changes errors, warnings, destructive actions, security or financial wording, accessibility labels, or operational instructions. Those messages stay clear and exact at every level. No text is sent to a remote service. · 搞笑等級永遠唔會改錯誤、警告、破壞性操作、安全／金融文字、無障礙標籤或者操作指示；任何級別都保持清楚準確，亦唔會將文字傳去遠端服務。
